## 3. Compliance with JMIR Editorial Policies

1. **Mandatory File Format:** JMIR strictly requires MS Word (`.docx`). LaTeX is not accepted directly in the editorial system. `JMIR_Manuscript.docx` has been formatted and tested. A companion compiled `main.pdf` is also generated for archival inspection.
2. **Title Requirement:** JMIR requires the study design to be explicitly declared in the subtitle:
   > *"Federated Personalized Drift-Aware Attention Framework for Early Sepsis Forecasting Across Multi-Center Intensive Care Units: Retrospective Model Development and Validation Study"*
3. **Structured Abstract:** Exactly formatted under five subheadings: **Background**, **Objective**, **Methods**, **Results**, **Conclusions**. Total length is **447 words** (strictly under the JMIR $\le 450$ words threshold).
4. **Manuscript Length:** Total word count is **~6,800 words**, well within JMIR's recommended range of 5,000–7,500 words for Original Papers.
5. **Tables and Figures:** JMIR limits submissions to 5–8 tables and figures. This manuscript includes **4 tables** and **6 figures** (10 visual assets total, matching the recommended ceiling).
6. **Reference Style:** AMA 11th Edition numbered references (`[1]`, `[2]`), with exact DOIs, PMIDs, and journal titles abbreviated per PubMed standards.
7. **P-Value Formatting:** Formatted per JMIR guidelines with capitalized italics without leading zero (e.g., *$P<.001$*).
8. **Required Back Matter:** Includes explicit sections for:
   - *Acknowledgments*
   - *Funding Statement* (Self-funded B.Tech Capstone Project, no commercial funding)
   - *Conflicts of Interest* (Declared none)
   - *Data Availability Statement* (PhysioNet 2019 open benchmark and GitHub code repository)
   - *Authors' Contributions* (CRediT Statement covering all 8 contributors)
   - *Abbreviations* (23 defined acronyms)

## 5. Step-by-Step JMIR Submission Protocol

1. Navigate to [JMIR Author Submission Portal](https://www.jmir.org/author) and log in with your ORCID credentials.
2. Select **Journal of Medical Internet Research (JMIR)** as the target venue.
3. Choose Article Type: **Original Paper**.
4. Paste the structured abstract (447 words) into the metadata fields.
5. Upload primary manuscript: select `JMIR_Manuscript.docx`.
6. Upload Cover Letter: select `JMIR_Cover_Letter.docx`.
7. Upload Supplementary / Checklist: select `JMIR_Submission_Checklist.docx`.
8. Enter Co-Author ORCIDs and affiliations.
9. Enter the 4 suggested peer reviewers.
10. Confirm cascading peer-review preference (allows transfer to *JMIR Medical Informatics* if deemed more focused).
11. Review submission proof and complete submission.
